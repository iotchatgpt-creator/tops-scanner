End-to-End Tracking

Demonstrating the Full Textile Lifecycle

Clean Flow

Moving fresh textiles from Buffer Inventory to Resort Storage.

1. Allocate

Assign Available metros to specific delivery orders.

2. Transport

Load metros onto trucks (Pickup) and dispatch (In Transit).

3. Delivery

Receive at destination (At Storage) making them ready for use.

Soiled Flow

Returning used textiles back to the laundry plant.

1. Mark Soiled

Textiles used; mark the metro as Soiled at its location.

2. Return Transit

Scan during pickup to move to Soiled Transit heading to dock.

3. Processing

Arrive At Plant, undergo Cleaning, and return to Available.
